## Brand & Style

This design system defines an operational ecosystem for advanced Physical AI urban computing and autonomous intersection management. The aesthetic merges precision deep-tech engineering with real-time dynamic spatial intelligence. The design tone is authoritative, hyper-focused, and luminous against dark operational environments—reminiscent of high-end mission control rooms and computational simulation software.

The visual execution relies on a refined dark mode foundation paired with Glassmorphism and Neon Data-Luminescence. Visual priority is dictated by kinetic signals: semi-translucent slate surfaces, laser-fine structural dividers, and targeted cyan/electric-blue data accents that signify live computational flow, throughput anomalies, and AI predictive paths. The experience balances intense data density with zero visual noise, providing operators and traffic engineers with instant cognitive clarity.

## Colors

The color palette is architected for continuous dark-environment monitoring with high visual contrast and zero eye fatigue.

- **Foundational Surfaces:** Canvas base uses deep space navy (`#0B0F19`), shifting up to elevated container surfaces in slate obsidian (`#111827`) and muted analytical surfaces (`#1E293B`).
- **Signal & Illumination Palette:** 
  - **Electric Cyan (`#00D2FF`):** The primary signal token. Applied to real-time agent trajectories, active AI inference states, peak flow vectors, and critical metric highlights.
  - **Neon Core Blue (`#0066FF`):** The structural primary. Used for primary user interactions, foundational focus indicators, and confirmed control states.
  - **Atmospheric Blue (`#38BDF8`):** Serves as an auxiliary accent for micro-charts, telemetry badges, and sensor ray lines.
- **Data Status Tokens:**
  - Optimal / Free Flow: `#10B981` (Emerald Core)
  - Congestion / Warning: `#F59E0B` (Amber Radiant)
  - Bottleneck / Incident Critical: `#EF4444` (Crimson Pulse)
- **Text & High-Contrast Accents:** Pure optical white (`#FFFFFF`) for primary data outputs and metric figures, transitioning to cool slate silver (`#94A3B8`) for labels and structural indices, and muted graphite (`#475569`) for disabled states and inactive grid lines.

## Typography

The type system prioritizes technical legibility and rapid numerical scanning across complex dashboards:

- **Display & Headlines (Space Grotesk):** Provides an architectural, futuristic authority for major dashboards, intersection IDs, and zone titles without sacrificing optical geometry.
- **Body & Controls (Inter):** Guarantees high-density readability for configuration panels, alerts, and analytical prose under variable lighting conditions.
- **Telemetry & Metrics (JetBrains Mono):** Reserved for sensor readings, coordinates, velocity matrices, confidence intervals, and timestamps. Monospaced tabular alignment prevents visual jitter during live millisecond streaming updates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a dense 12-column dynamic computational grid that reflows across operational viewports:

- **Desktop & Command Displays (1440px+):** 12-column grid, 2rem margins, 1.5rem gutters. Supports multi-pane telemetry layouts: fixed-width lateral telemetric toolbars (320px), central real-time map or sensor canvas (fluid), and flexible analytical side-drawers (380px).
- **Tablet & Compact Terminals (768px – 1439px):** 8-column layout, 1.5rem margins, 1rem gutters. Side-drawers convert to stacked collapsible analytical sheets.
- **Mobile Handheld (320px – 767px):** 4-column layout, 1rem margins, 1rem gutters. All analytical overlays convert to vertically scrollable cards and swipable KPI decks.

Spacing scales systematically via an 8pt foundational baseline (with a 4pt sub-grid for dense operational metrics). Internal component paddings prioritize high density without visual encroachment.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is established using layered spatial luminance rather than traditional opaque drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Solid background (`#0B0F19`) acting as the infinite sensor coordinate space.
- **Level 1 (Structural Containers & Data Grid Panels):** Translucent deep slate (`rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.75)`) combined with a 16px background blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) and a delicate outline of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Operational Overlays & Floating Hubs):** Elevated glassy containers (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.8)`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, bounded by `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)` and a low-frequency ambient cyan halo (`box-shadow: 0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Level 3 (Alerts, Active Modals, & High-Priority Interventions):** Semi-opaque composite (`rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.95)`) bounded by a vibrant neon stroke (`1px solid #00D2FF`) with a directed photon glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.25), 0 12px 48px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`).

## Shapes

The geometric architecture relies on disciplined, low-radius softness (`0.25rem` / 4px base radius) for functional controls, data matrices, and visual containers. This preserves the precision aesthetic of high-tech instrumentation without the harshness of zero-radius brutalism.

- **Standard Surfaces & Metric Containers:** 4px radius (`rounded-sm`) to 8px radius (`rounded-lg`) for clean alignment against dense data grids.
- **Pill Exceptions:** High-salience AI features, live telemetry indicators, status states, and dynamic filters explicitly leverage full pill radiuses (`9999px`) to immediately contrast against rigid analytical matrices.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Cybernetic Button:** Background in vivid electric cyan (`#00D2FF`), text in deep navy (`#0B0F19`), weight 600. Includes subtle perimeter radiance (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.35)`). Hover state shifts surface to `#38BDF8` with expanded glow.
- **Secondary Ghost / Outline Button:** Surface transparent, border `1px solid rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.4)`, text `#00D2FF`. Hover triggers `background: rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Destructive / Emergency Stop Button:** Border `1px solid #EF4444`, background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.1)`, text `#EF4444`. Glow triggers crimson warning halo on hover.

### Badges & Pill Chips
- **AI Inference Badges:** Full pill geometry (`rounded-full`), padded `0.25rem 0.75rem`. Background `rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.15)`, text `#00D2FF`, border `1px solid rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.3)`. Features an integrated pulsating dot indicating real-time edge processing.
- **Metric Filter Chips:** Monospaced typography, pill form factor, inactive state rendered in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` with `#94A3B8` text. Active state adopts solid `#0066FF` with white text.

### Telemetry Cards & Metric Panels
- **Container Structure:** Dark translucent glass (`rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.75)`), 12px internal padding, micro-hairline border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`).
- **Typography Arrangement:** Small monospaced category header (`label-sm`, `#94A3B8`) anchored above large high-contrast numerical readouts (`headline-md`, `#FFFFFF`) with inline micro trend arrows and cyan delta vectors.

### Data Lists & Analytical Feeds
- **Row Styling:** Alternating subtle zebra striping using `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`. Left edge houses an active status stripe (2px width) tinted according to intersection flow status. Hover highlights row with `rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.04)`.
- **Dividers:** 1px hairline borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).

### Form Inputs & Parameter Sliders
- **Text Inputs:** Low-elevation slate field (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.5)`), border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, placeholder text `#475569`, text `#FFFFFF`. Active focus transitions border instantly to `#00D2FF` with a focused field glow (`0 0 0 1px #00D2FF, 0 0 12px rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.2)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp 3px radius checks and circular radios. Unchecked: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. Checked: `#00D2FF` fill with pure `#0B0F19` iconography.

### Specialized AI & Traffic Elements
- **Intersection Vector Node:** Dynamic SVG radar/crosshair markers equipped with real-time directional flow arrows, color-coded by density and vehicle-type inference (transit, freight, autonomous fleets, pedestrian cross-flows).
- **Throughput Gauge Ring:** Radial micro-gauges displaying real-time AI signal optimization efficiency percentage, rendered in glowing cyan-to-blue vector arcs against a muted dark track.
